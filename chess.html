Quick reference

Know your pieces.

Each piece has a unique movement pattern and strategic purpose.

♔

King

Moves one square in any direction. Protect your king at all costs.

♕

Queen

Moves any number of squares horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

♖

Rook

Moves any distance horizontally or vertically, provided the path is clear.

♗

Bishop

Moves diagonally across the board, staying on the same square colour.

♘

Knight

Moves in an L-shape and can jump over other pieces.

♙

Pawn

Moves forward, normally one square, but captures diagonally.

♔!

Check

Your king is attacked. You must make a legal move to escape check.

♚

Checkmate

Your king is in check with no legal escape. The game ends immediately.
